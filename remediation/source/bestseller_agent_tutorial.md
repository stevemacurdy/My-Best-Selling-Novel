# BESTSELLER BOOK AGENT — COMPLETE TUTORIAL GUIDE
## From Blank Page to Published Bestseller in 12 Steps

---

## GETTING STARTED

When you first open the Bestseller Book Agent, you'll see your **Book Library** — a collection of all your book projects. Click **+ New Book** to start your first one.

The agent guides you through 12 sequential steps. Each step unlocks the next as you progress. You can always go back to previous steps using the navigation bar at the top.

Your work auto-saves after every change. Close the tab, come back tomorrow — everything is right where you left it.

---

## STEP 1: GENRE SCANNER

**What it does:** AI researches the current book market and shows you 12 high-opportunity genres with demand levels, competition, reader profiles, trending tropes, and opportunity scores (1-5).

**How to use it:**
1. Click **Scan Genres** — the AI analyzes 2026 bestseller trends
2. Browse the genre cards — each shows demand (HIGH/VERY HIGH), competition (LOW/MODERATE), and opportunity score
3. Click **Show 12 More** for additional genres the AI finds
4. Or type any genre in the custom input and click **Research This Genre**
5. Click a genre card to select it → you'll advance to Book Setup

**Pro tip:** Look for genres with VERY HIGH demand and LOW competition — these are your best opportunities. The opportunity score (1-5) already factors this in.

---

## STEP 2: BOOK SETUP

**What it does:** Captures the essential information about your book that every subsequent step uses.

**Fields to fill in:**
- **Working Title** — doesn't have to be final, you can change it anytime
- **Subtitle** — especially important for non-fiction (shows up on Amazon)
- **Author Name** — used for copyright page, cover text, and all exports
- **Target Word Count** — pre-filled based on your genre's typical range
- **Target Reader** — who specifically is this book for?
- **Impact** — what transformation should readers experience?
- **Unique Angle** — what makes YOUR take on this topic different?
- **Book Type** — Standalone, Series (Book 1), or Series (Continuation)

**If you're writing a series:** The Series Bible field appears. Use it to track characters, locations, rules, timeline, and relationships across books. This gets passed to the AI during chapter writing to maintain continuity.

---

## STEP 3: UPLOAD & ORGANIZE

**What it does:** Takes whatever you have — a finished manuscript, a rough outline, or a pile of unorganized notes — and processes it appropriately.

**Upload your content:**
- Drag and drop files (.pdf, .docx, .txt, .md)
- Or paste text directly into the textarea
- Multiple files can be uploaded — they concatenate

**Then choose your manuscript type:**

### Path A: Unorganized Content (📝)
For notes, drafts, research, ideas all mixed together.
- Click **Organize My Manuscript**
- AI reads the entire manuscript (handles 200K+ words via chunked analysis)
- Returns: recommended chapter breaks, flow issues, content gaps, strategy assessment
- The AI tries to split your actual text at the recommended break points
- Click **Accept & Continue** when satisfied

### Path B: Outline Only (📋)
For chapter headings and brief notes, but chapters aren't written yet.
- After you generate an outline (Step 4), come back here
- Each chapter shows **12 tone selectors**: Conversational, Entertaining, Instructional, Warning, Scary, Exciting, Persuasive, Dissuading, Tear-jerker, Inspirational, Author's Voice, Humorous
- Select one or more tones per chapter
- Click **AI Write This Chapter** — it writes in rich detail matching your selected tones
- Progress shows as each chapter is written

### Path C: Written with Chapters (📖)
For manuscripts that are mostly done with chapter headings.
- The agent auto-detects chapter boundaries (Chapter 1:, Part 2:, Section 3:, etc.)
- Shows each detected chapter with word count and preview
- Click **Split & Load** — one click populates every chapter's editing field
- No AI involved, no truncation, works at any manuscript size

**Voice Analysis:** Before leaving this step, click **Analyze My Writing Voice**. The AI reads your text and creates a style profile that every future AI function uses to match your writing voice.

---

## STEP 4: OUTLINE BUILDER

**What it does:** Creates or refines your book's chapter structure.

**How to use it:**
1. Click **Generate Outline** — AI creates 12-20 chapters with titles, purposes, key points, word targets, and end hooks
2. **Edit any title** — click on a chapter title and type your own
3. **Edit purposes** — click the purpose field under each title
4. **Delete a chapter** — click the ✕ button (remaining chapters auto-renumber)
5. **Add a chapter** — click **+ Add Chapter** at the bottom
6. **Add section headers** — click the **§+** button on any chapter to add a section divider above it (e.g., "Part I: The Foundation")

**Section headers** are for books organized into parts or sections. They appear as gold dividers and are included in your manuscript export.

---

## STEP 5: FRONT & BACK MATTER

**What it does:** Professional framing sections that bookend your content.

**Front Matter (5 sections):** Dedication, Foreword, Preface, Introduction, Epigraph
**Back Matter (7 sections):** Author's Note, Acknowledgments, About the Author, Also By, Newsletter CTA, Review Request, Next Book Excerpt

**How to use it:**
1. Switch between **Front Matter** and **Back Matter** tabs
2. For each section, either:
   - Write your own text directly in the textarea
   - Click **AI Draft** — the AI auto-fills the field with a professional draft
3. Edit the AI draft as needed — it's directly in the textarea
4. Tips appear below after drafting (💡 hints)

**Copyright Page:** Click **Auto-Generate** — it fills in from your author name, detects fiction vs. non-fiction, includes ISBN placeholders.

**Most important section:** The Newsletter CTA. This converts 3-8% of readers into email subscribers. The AI prompt is designed to create irresistible bonus content offers.

---

## STEP 6: CHAPTER GUIDE

**What it does:** Per-chapter AI guidance with an interactive issue-resolution system.

**How to use it:**
1. Click a chapter in the sidebar
2. Click **Get Guidance** — AI reads your actual chapter text and returns:
   - 🎯 Opening strategy
   - 💫 Emotional arc
   - ⏱️ Pacing notes
   - 🏆 Bestseller tip
   - 📝 Scenes needed
   - ⚠️ Things to Avoid

**The Avoid System (unique feature):**
Each "Avoid" item is interactive. For each issue:
- **"Not an Issue"** — dismisses it (struck through with green border)
- **"Yes, Fix This"** — two options appear:
  - **"I'll Fix It Myself"** — takes you to the Write step
  - **"AI Fix in My Voice"** — AI reads your entire book context (story arc, previous chapters, series bible, voice profile) and rewrites ONLY the flagged section. It never scrambles facts.
- After AI fix: **Apply to Chapter** inserts the fix, or **Regenerate** tries again

## STEP 7: WRITE & RECORD

**What it does:** Two modes — writing chapters and recording your audiobook.

### Write Mode (✍️)
- Select a chapter from the sidebar (green dot = written)
- Type directly in the textarea
- Click **AI Write** or **AI Rewrite** — generates/rewrites in your voice with continuity from previous chapters
- Word count and progress bar track your target
- Navigate chapters with ← → buttons

### Record Mode (🎙️)
- Select a chapter from the sidebar (pink dot = recorded)
- Your chapter text shows as a read-along teleprompter
- Click **Upload Chapter Audio** — pick a pre-recorded .mp3, .wav, .m4a, .webm, .ogg, .aac, or .flac file
- **Play** to listen back, **Replace** to swap, **Delete** to remove
- When all chapters are recorded, **Download All** exports separate files

**Recording tips** show before your first upload — mic recommendations, room treatment, pacing target (155 words/minute), and more.

**ACX Checklist** — 10 items covering the exact technical specs Audible requires.

---

## STEP 8: AI REVIEW

**What it does:** Four modes of editorial feedback on any written chapter.

**Modes:**
- 🔍 **Proofread** — finds errors with original/corrected/reason
- ✨ **Embellish** — suggests sensory details and enhancements
- ⚔️ **Critique** — harsh bestseller-readiness score (1-10) with ranked action items
- 🔄 **Rewrite** — completely rewrites the chapter in your voice

**How to use it:**
1. Choose a mode
2. Select a chapter (only written chapters appear)
3. Review the results
4. For Rewrite mode: click **Accept Rewrite** to replace your chapter with the improved version

---

## STEP 9: BOOK DESCRIPTION

**What it does:** Generates four pieces of sales copy.

**What you get:**
- **Amazon Description** — HTML formatted with Hook→Stakes→Promise→Comps→CTA formula (4,000 char max)
- **Hook Line** — one sentence for ads
- **One-Liner** — social media pitch
- **Back Cover Blurb** — 150-200 words for print

All fields are editable. Character counter shows on the Amazon description.

## STEP 10: PUBLISHING SETUP

**What it does:** Six tabs of Amazon optimization and audiobook planning.

### 🔑 Keywords
7 backend keyword boxes with byte counter (249 bytes each). AI fills them with tropes, comp authors, synonyms — never repeating words from your title.

### 📂 Categories
3 Amazon subcategories with competition levels and daily sales needed for Top 10. Warns about ghost categories.

### 💰 Pricing
Launch → Transition → Long-term strategy with royalty math at each tier. Print pricing included.

### 🎧 Audiobook
Estimated length, Audible retail pricing (they set it), production options (human narrator, AI narration, royalty share), distribution strategy, narrator guidance, and launch timing.

### 📚 Comps
5 comparable bestsellers with why they're similar and ad targeting notes.

### 📣 Ads
3 Amazon Ads campaigns (comp title targeting, category keywords, author targeting) with suggested bids, daily budgets, and kill criteria.

## STEP 11: COVER DESIGN

**What it does:** Four ways to manage your book covers.

### 📤 Upload Single Cover
Drag and drop your cover. If it's a full wrap (front + spine + back), text fields appear for each zone — auto-populated from your book setup.

### 📚 Upload Series Covers
Drag all cover files at once. They sort by filename and assign to Book 1, 2, 3... Use the ± stepper for any number of books.

### 📁 Upload Cover Folders
Select a folder with subfolders per book. Agent auto-detects format from filenames (ebook, print, audiobook, social, etc.). Reassign format with dropdown if detection is wrong.

### 🎨 Design Concepts
Color palette picker, spine view (shelf mockup), and BookTok mockup scene for cover reveal posts.

## MULTI-BOOK LIBRARY

Click **← My Books** at any time to return to your library. You can:
- Start new books
- Resume existing books
- See word counts and last modified dates
- Delete books (with confirmation)

Each book stores separately — your 200K-word epic doesn't interfere with your 40K self-help book.

## TIPS FOR BEST RESULTS

1. **Fill in Book Setup completely** — every field you fill gives the AI more context
2. **Analyze your voice early** — do this in Step 3 before any AI writing
3. **Use the Chapter Guide** — the Avoid system catches issues you won't see yourself
4. **Write first, AI review second** — the agent is an editor, not a ghost writer
5. **Generate descriptions AFTER chapters are written** — the AI uses your content for better copy
6. **Do keywords and categories LAST** — they need your final title and description
7. **Export the manuscript frequently** — keep local backups
8. **Use the health score** — it tells you exactly what's missing before launch
